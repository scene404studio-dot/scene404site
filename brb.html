Meet BRB.

A little character with a world of its own.

BRB is the first product from SCENE404 studio — an exploration of what digital things can become when they have personality, behavior, and a reason to return.

Discover BRB

MORE THAN AN INTERFACE

A character you can come back to.

We are interested in the space between a useful product and something that feels familiar.

BRB is where we begin exploring that idea through character, interaction, and small digital moments.

THE IDEA

Useful isn't enough.

The things we keep are rarely defined by function alone. They become familiar. They gather meaning. We remember them.

BRB is an experiment in bringing more of that feeling into digital products.

IN THE MAKING

One product. A world to explore.

Character, objects, interactions, and details are all part of the same idea. We are building BRB one piece at a time.
