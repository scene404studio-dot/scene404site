Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information SCENE404 studio may collect through this website and how it may be used.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the SCENE404 studio website and information submitted through its forms. In this policy, “SCENE404 studio,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the studio operating this website.

2. Information you provide

If you join our waitlist or contact us, you may provide information such as your email address and the contents of your message.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our general website forms.

3. How we use information

We may use submitted information to:

  • manage waitlist requests;
  • send studio, product, and release updates you requested;
  • respond to questions and support requests;
  • operate, maintain, and protect the website; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Forms and service providers

Website forms may be processed by third-party service providers, including Formspree. Information submitted through a form may be handled by that provider under its own privacy terms and policies.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers. Review their policies before submitting information if you would like more detail about their practices.

5. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. We may disclose information to service providers that help operate the website or deliver requested communications, or when disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights, or address security issues.

Certain privacy laws define “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising more broadly than an ordinary monetary sale. Your rights under applicable law are described below.

6. Cookies and analytics

The website or its hosting and service providers may use essential technical storage, logs, or similar technologies to operate and secure the site. If analytics or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing requested updates, maintaining records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

8. Your choices

If you receive email updates from us, you may unsubscribe using the unsubscribe link in the message, where available. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

9. California and other state privacy rights

Depending on where you live and whether the applicable law covers our activities, you may have rights to know about, access, correct, or delete certain personal information, obtain a portable copy, or opt out of certain forms of targeted advertising, sale, or sharing.

To submit a privacy request, email hello@scene404.studio with the subject “Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your request before acting on it. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

The text “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” in the footer is provided as a privacy-related notice. It should be connected to an appropriate opt-out process if applicable law requires one; the text alone does not process an opt-out request.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no website, transmission, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children’s privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, our products, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its updated date.
