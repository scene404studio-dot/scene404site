Privacy Policy

SCENE404 studio respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the BRB website or join the waitlist.

Information you provide

When you join the waitlist, you may provide your email address. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the form.

How information is used

Your email address may be used to send BRB updates, early-access information, launch timing, and related SCENE404 studio announcements.

Service providers

The waitlist form is processed through Formspree. Information submitted through the form may be processed by that service in accordance with its own policies. Website hosting and other technical services may also process information necessary to operate the website.

Sharing and retention

SCENE404 studio does not sell your personal information. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to manage the waitlist, provide requested updates, and meet applicable obligations.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about your information or request that your waitlist email be removed.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@scene404.studio.
