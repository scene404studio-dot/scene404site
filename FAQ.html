A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

A little more about the studio, what we're making, and how to keep up with what's next.

What is SCENE404 studio?

SCENE404 is an independent studio exploring digital products, characters, and worlds worth keeping around.

What is BRB?

BRB is the first product from SCENE404 studio. It is an exploration of digital character, interaction, and the feeling of returning to something familiar.

Is BRB available yet?

BRB is in development. Join the waitlist to receive updates when there is something new to share.

What does joining the waitlist mean?

It lets us send you occasional updates from the studio, including news about BRB and future projects. Joining the list does not guarantee early access or a particular release date.

Will SCENE404 make other products?

BRB is the first product, not the limit of the studio. SCENE404 is built to explore ideas across digital products, character, interaction, and world-building.

How often will you email me?

We aim to send relevant studio updates rather than frequent promotional emails. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link, where provided.

How do you use my email address?

We use information submitted through our forms to manage waitlist requests and communicate relevant updates. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

How can I contact the studio?

Visit our Contact page or email hello@scene404.studio.
