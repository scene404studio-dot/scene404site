01 / The World

More than a character on a screen.

BRB is designed as a small digital environment, not just a character to look at. The experience brings together a recognizable companion, interactive moments, and a world that can feel a little different when you come back.

The idea is simple: give curiosity somewhere to go. Look around, interact, play, and find your own reasons to return.

02 / Interactions

Little things to do. Little things to discover.

The BRB experience is being developed around everyday interactions that make its digital world feel active and personal.

03 / Small Moments

A world that gives you a reason to return.

Not every digital experience needs to be about finishing something. Sometimes, the appeal is in the little things: a new interaction, a playful discovery, or simply seeing what has changed.

BRB is being designed around that feeling — a digital world with room for curiosity, play, and moments that become familiar over time.

04 / The Idea

Made to be revisited.

We spend so much time moving between digital products that are designed for the next tap, the next scroll, and the next thing.

BRB explores a different idea: a small digital world with its own character, details worth noticing, and an experience you can return to in your own time.

It is a new project from SCENE404 studio, currently in development. We are building the world one detail at a time.
