ABOUT THE STUDIO

Things worth keeping around.

SCENE404 is an independent studio exploring what digital products can feel like when they become more than tools.

We make products, characters, and worlds shaped by one belief: usefulness matters, but it is not the whole story.

Explore the world

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Useful isn't enough.

The digital world is full of things that work. Fewer things give us a reason to remember them, return to them, or make them part of our everyday lives.

We are interested in that difference — the space between function and familiarity.

WHAT WE MAKE

Products with a point of view.

Our work brings together digital product thinking, character, visual systems, interaction, and world-building.

Each project begins with a question about how something could work — and what could make it worth keeping.

Meet BRB

THE MISSION

To make things people want to keep around.

We want to create digital experiences that feel considered, recognizable, and worth returning to — without adding noise just for the sake of attention.
