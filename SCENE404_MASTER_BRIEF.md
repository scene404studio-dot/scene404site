# SCENE404 studio — Master Website Brief

## Core positioning
SCENE404 studio is an independent creative studio making digital products, characters, and original worlds with a clear point of view. BRB is Chapter 01 / the first product, not the entire studio identity.

**Studio thought:** Useful isn't enough.
**Promise:** Things worth keeping around.
**Voice:** Nonchalant, observant, dry/warm, slightly strange. Say less; mean enough.

## Visual system
- White background, near-black typography, restrained gray, fine dividers, generous whitespace.
- Burgundy is an occasional editorial accent; do not turn the site into a pink theme.
- Avoid beige-heavy styling, kawaii cuteness, neon cyberpunk, glassy effects, clutter, and generic startup language.
- Product imagery should feel considered and editorial, not oversized without purpose.
- Use the exact brand spelling `SCENE404 studio`.

## Page roles
- `index.html` / The Studio: establish the studio and point of view; include the blueprint/concept visual and a concise introduction to BRB.
- `brb.html`: product page with a swipeable/clickable edition carousel and a concise explanation of BRB.
- `world.html`: character collection grid; compact cards, clear names and short descriptions. No One in a Million feature here.
- `about.html`: studio story and founder note. Begin with a blueprint/concept image. Feature `brb-one-in-a-million.jpeg` here only.

## Asset rules
- Images live in the repository root unless the site is intentionally reorganized.
- Preserve exact filename capitalization and extensions.
- Do not use the same hero artwork on multiple pages without explicit approval.
- Do not delete existing assets as part of a visual refresh.

## Motion
- Keep movement subtle and purposeful: carousel transitions, slight image reveal, gentle character tilt on hover.
- Support reduced-motion preferences.
- Never claim an animation works until it has been tested in the browser.
